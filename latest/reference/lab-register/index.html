    Lab register¶

    Every page of this site that says how a device behaves rests on a claim that port49's lab can test. Each claim has an ID, an L-number such as the ones linked throughout the site, and this page lists them all. A claim is one question about a device or a protocol, for example whether a switch shows a message port49 sends with a denial, and the lab answers it on real device images.

    • Open means the lab has not finished measuring the claim. The documentation never asserts an open claim as fact: where a page mentions one, it says the behavior is unmeasured, or it cites the results note for the platform it measured. A row can be open while one platform is measured and others are not.
    • Closed means a lab test passed and a results note records the platform, the image version, what was observed and where the capture is. The note is the evidence.

    A results note wins over any page: when a note and a page disagree, the page is wrong and is corrected to match the note. The register itself is section 15 of the policy-engine spec, and the rows below are shortened from it. Follow the spec link for a row's full wording.
