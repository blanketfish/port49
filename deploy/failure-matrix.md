# Failure matrix

Design §12 as an operator reads it: what the device does, what you see, what you do, and the evidence
that says so. Every row names a subtest of `tests/compose/failmatrix_test.go`
(`TestFailureMatrix/<row>`, run by `make failure-matrix` and the `failmatrix` CI job), an existing test,
or a closed lab row. `TestEveryMatrixRowHasEvidence` fails when a row of design §12 is missing here or has
no evidence.

## At 3am

| You see | What the devices do | Do this | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| port49d is up and its store is failing (`port49_store_health_failures` rising) | port49d answers a token login `ERROR` `store_unavailable` and every command is denied, for up to 5.5 s (`close_after` 5 x `interval` 1 s + `call_timeout`), then closes TCP 49. **Cisco IOS XE treats that `ERROR` as final, so break-glass is refused for those 5.5 s.** **Junos under the reference `authentication-order tacplus` (Decision 3) does the same.** After the close, devices find the connection refused and fall to their local method | Wait the 5.5 s, or stop port49d to get there at once. Then fix the store | `port49_store_health_failures_close` |
| SR Linux, same case | Not yet measured: L87 decides whether break-glass waits for the close, as above. The reference does not ship until L87 (b) passes | Filled in with the L87 result (plan 12c Task 11) | |
| `port49_redemption_restore_guarded` is 1 | port49d refuses every token login with an `ERROR`, which IOS XE and Junos under `tacplus` alone treat as final, so break-glass is refused too. The guard lifts when the brokers purge, or when port49d's own clock passes the latest expiry among the token records the restored store holds (`internal/port49d/restoreguard.go`), which port49d's guard line and `port49_redemption_restore_guard_until_timestamp_seconds` give. That is at most one token lifetime (15m) after the snapshot's last mint, so a snapshot older than that is never guarded. If the scan fails, a record does not decode, or an expiry is further ahead than a token lifetime plus 5m of clock skew, no bound is armed and only the purge lifts it. While the brokers are down, break-glass on those devices is refused by port49d's FAIL even after the guard lifts | Start the brokers: they purge and the guard lifts. If the brokers cannot start, **stop port49d**: devices then find the connection refused and fall to their local method. Revocations made after the snapshot are lost by any restore: re-issue them | `port49_redemption_restore_guarded` (10m) |
| TCP 49 refused at once | The device treats port49 as down and uses its local method | Start port49d, or leave it stopped until the store is back | |
